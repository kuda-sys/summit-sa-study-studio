SUMMIT ACADEMY — SA TEST STUDIO v1.3
=====================================

How to use
----------
1. Extract the complete ZIP folder.
2. Open index.html in a modern desktop browser (Chrome or Edge recommended).
3. Keep the assets folder next to index.html. Do not move index.html on its own.
4. Student progress is saved in that browser on that device.

What's included
---------------
- Mock SA Test and Quick Practice
- Grammar, comparatives/superlatives, Smart Everyday English, sentence building,
  and New Crown English Series 2 Lessons 4–6 vocabulary
- Reading comprehension missions and Listening Studio video exercises
- Summit Academy branding and bundled media assets
- Speaking Gym microphone recording and playback controls
- Longer model answers in Speaking Gym and Writing Workshop
- Browser text-to-speech for model answers, preferring a female-sounding British
  English voice when the browser/operating system provides one

Smart Everyday English
----------------------
Each session contains exactly 10 multiple-choice questions, with a mix of direct
synonym/formal-alternative questions and sentence-replacement questions. The
question order and answer options are shuffled each time.

Audio behavior
--------------
- A randomly selected relaxing track loops during question-answering activities.
- The supplied click sound plays on buttons, tabs, links, and clickable learning
  path cards/images.
- The correct-answer sound plays at the end of a set when the score is above 70%.
  The wrong-answer sound plays when the score is 70% or lower.
- No page-turn sound is used.

Speaking recording and voice playback
-------------------------------------
The browser will ask for microphone permission. Recording requires a modern
browser that supports MediaRecorder and microphone access; microphone access can
depend on browser security settings and how the local app is opened. Recordings
are kept in the current browser session for playback and are not uploaded.
Text-to-speech voice availability varies by device. The app prefers a British
English voice with a female-sounding voice name when one is available, then
falls back to another British English voice or the browser's en-GB setting.

Question bank
-------------
The generated question pool contains 5,080 questions: 1,000 each in five practice
strands and 80 reading-comprehension questions. Read QUESTION_BANK_QA.txt for the
automated checks and their limitations.

Important
---------
This is a static, local app. It is not a hosted multi-account school portal.
Progress is stored locally in the browser and is not synchronized between devices.
